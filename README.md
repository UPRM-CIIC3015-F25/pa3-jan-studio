# Balatro PA 3

## Bonuses

---

### **Mechanisms**
- Joker Modifiers: implemented Holographic, Foil and Polychrome Joker effects.
- **Holographic (purple):** +5 multiplier (13% chance of appearing in shop)
- **Polychrome (green border):** x1.25 multiplier (13% chance of appearing in shop)
- **Foil (blue border):** +25 chips (13% chance of appearing in shop)
- **One hand left warning:** messages the player when they only have one hand left

---

### **Design choices**
- Skin Selector  
- Gameover screen: when the player loses, the gameover screen takes over and quits the game  
- Golden border added to selected cards when playing a hand  
- Planet cards: redesign of the planet cards  

---

### **Music**
- Shop music  
- Game over sound effect  
- One hand left sound effect  
- Swoosh sound effect added when sorting cards either by rank or suit
